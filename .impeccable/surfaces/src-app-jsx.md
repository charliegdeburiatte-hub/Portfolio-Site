# Surface brief: home (single page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: recruiters and hiring managers for IT support roles, skimming in about 30 seconds, often on a phone; freelance clients second. Action: open the CV. Proof: Job Application Analyser v3.0.0 (live app and code), A+, Google IT Support, Tier 1-2 at VantageUAV.

Approved content decisions (2026-10-09): flagship shows top 5 tags on page (full list stays in data); remove "Professional Portfolio Website" card; remove footer status text and version label; CV is PDF view with PDF and Word downloads.

Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a glass lock screen you have just picked up, not a hero over a card grid. It refuses the standard split hero with a project grid underneath.

OWN-WORLD: Deep lagoon field (#08485F to #0A5C78) with one lit aqua pool, near-solid frost glass with refracting white rims and pointer-led specular light, Aero gloss title bars on anything that opens, iPhone 4 gloss pills, linen dock and brushed-metal rims on anything you press.

STORY: In one screen the visitor reads the name and role, sees availability, certs and experience as widgets, sees the flagship as a live notification they can open, and taps View CV. Scrolling, projects open as Aero windows, IT work first.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating glass capsule nav at top (name, Projects, About, Skills, Contact, CV). Centre column: name at lock-screen clock scale in white, role line, tagline; three frosted widgets in a row (stacked on phones); flagship notification below with Open app and View code; linen dock at the bottom holding amber View CV and metal Get in touch, in thumb reach.

FORM: Lock Screen, position 3 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key 2fa0fe46. Raises: single light-streak spine; one owned lagoon hue; status by shape and label; stable linkable addresses.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
